## Finite negative matrix

Every effect-bearing row uses its own explicitly selected scope. A failed or
uncertain scope is retained; the next row cannot reuse it after administrative
marker/socket cleanup. Bounded phase/public-category diagnostics are specified
before a run; raw private logs stay private.

| Row | Required behavior and independent evidence | Existing SOURCE evidence / installed remainder |
|---|---|---|

| Wrong input/authentication | Invalid bounded input or failed authentication precedes publisher/manager effects | Actual malformed CLI refusals and private archive controls; installed authentication row pending |

NEW/Committed, mixed/torn output and partial completion/disposition recovery are
not implemented by this first consumer. They remain mandatory before full
Restore/recovery in 0.9.9. Rejecting them does not close their acceptance.

## Reporting and stopping

Record the selected source, artifact hashes/features, environment/boot, original
exit and independent outcome for each row. Agent-entered VM confirmations are
agent-operated evidence, not human-attended acceptance. No source review, exit0,
receipt or screenshot alone closes installed recovery.

The new Stop/Start effect paths retain original child custody. Unchanged ordinary
admission/readiness observation helpers keep their existing query cleanup;
this card does not claim a new whole-observer or hard end-to-end deadline.

On uncertainty stop dependent effects, preserve the original available context
and report the exact unresolved phase. Do not kill a retained worker, remove
markers, boot/reset a failed image or manufacture cleanup. Administrative reset,
if separately authorized, is never product recovery acceptance.
