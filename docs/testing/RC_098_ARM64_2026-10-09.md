## Important boundaries

The initial source template was a historical development-fixture variant:
its only semantic difference from the bundled base was the two managed-DNS
flags, but their byte placement was not the supported producer transformation.
Backup correctly refused before publication; no data was silently normalized.
The positive used the ordinary routing-preset operation. Original private
profiles/template were retained and restored under a proven stopped runtime,
then byte equality of the original pair and Desired was verified after relaunch.
This fixture cleanup is **not** product Restore/recovery acceptance. The generic
input-refusal message does not explain the unsupported-template cause well;
do not widen the template allowlist or claim arbitrary custom-template support.

Before upgrade, the predecessor broker exited nonzero. Independent observation
proved no live process/listener, stored FD or private journal entry. Fixed-unit
Stop and clearing systemd's failed-unit bookkeeping preceded the unchanged
package guard. No DNS journal, enrollment, ownership or restore fence was removed;
this is not crash recovery acceptance of the new RC.

This review does not repeat deferred login/protocol/provider gates or claim a
new connected/egress result. Public assets/pins, anonymous release downloads,
clean guided provisioning and stable/main/Marketplace promotion remain separate.
Private captures, archive, passphrase and raw fixture data stay outside Git.
